About

For the past five years I have built the Higher Ed and Financial Aid platforms at The College Board, products that millions of students around the world depend on. I own the architecture of several of those services end to end, from the first design document to production operations.

The systems I design are distributed and serverless: AWS Lambda, API Gateway and DynamoDB, defined in TypeScript with AWS CDK and shipped through automated pipelines. I care about what keeps a system healthy after launch: observability, cost, failure modes, and the runbooks that let a team sleep.

I lead a team of six to eight engineers, from junior to senior, and I still write production code every week. Most recently my team shipped production AI features into those platforms, including an OCR document-processing pipeline and an AI chatbot. Before this I built financial and education products as a full-stack engineer, and I started my career writing C++ for CAD software. I hold a Master's in Software Engineering from George Mason University.
